HealthIA ONE starts from a simple idea: your health should not reset every time a conversation ends. Patients live with fragmented information across symptoms, laboratory files, medications, appointments, devices, and community resources. A normal chatbot can discuss one fragment. HealthIA is designed to carry unfinished health work forward until there is a durable, evidence-backed outcome.

This is the exact candidate running on Google Cloud. The patient signs in through the real HealthIA authentication flow. The interface follows the browser language, while the clinical conversation follows the language the patient is actually using.

The patient begins naturally, describing urinary symptoms. HealthIA does not immediately turn one sentence into a rigid form. It responds as a person first. When enough detail is present, Google ADK activates the bounded clinical workflow and Gemini 3.5 Flash generates exactly five context-specific questions. The questions appear one at a time, preserving prior answers so the conversation moves forward instead of restarting.

Next, the patient asks HealthIA to open Results. The chat becomes an operating surface: the request is converted into a deterministic, allow-listed interface action rather than an arbitrary model-generated selector.

The patient uploads a synthetic clinical PDF. HealthIA stores the original bytes first in private Google Cloud Storage. Only then does Gemini perform bounded multimodal extraction. The structured result is persisted in Firestore, linked to the original document, and connected to the longitudinal clinical state. If the evidence cannot be read reliably, HealthIA fails closed instead of inventing findings.

Now the patient asks HealthIA to explain the exact result that was just uploaded. This creates a Taskmaster mission. The mission is not marked complete because a language model produced a paragraph. It closes only after the persisted result and the original evidence are found and linked to the mission. That is the difference between chat memory and durable work.

HealthIA can also turn understanding itself into a workflow. We call this HealthIA Explain. The patient asks for a private video explaining the glucose result. HealthIA resolves the language of that request, selects only authorized topic-relevant evidence, creates a bounded storyboard, narrates it with Gemini 2.5 Pro Text-to-Speech, and can enrich a generic PHI-free scene with Veo 3.1 Fast. Exact patient values remain on controlled HealthIA cards. Veo never receives the patient name, medication list, laboratory values, identifiers, or other patient-specific facts. The generated video is stored privately and attached to the same patient-scoped state.

The patient can watch that explanation directly inside HealthIA. This is not a decorative video button. It is another durable mission with authorization, evidence boundaries, generation, validation, private storage, and a real media outcome.

Finally, the patient logs out and signs in again. The result, original document, completed mission, and longitudinal state are still there. Continuity survives the browser session because Firestore is the canonical patient state and the original evidence remains in private Cloud Storage.

The backend shown here is the exact candidate on Cloud Run, using Gemini 3.5 Flash on Vertex AI, Google ADK, Firestore, private Cloud Storage, Secret Manager, Gemini Text-to-Speech, and Veo. HealthIA ONE does not try to replace clinical judgment. It uses AI where interpretation is valuable, deterministic logic where precision matters, and explicit human consent where the decision belongs to the patient.

HealthIA ONE: your health never starts over.
