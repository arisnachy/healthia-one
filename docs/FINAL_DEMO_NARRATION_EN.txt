HealthIA ONE starts from a simple idea: your health should not reset every time a conversation ends. Patients live with fragmented information across laboratory files, images, medications, appointments, devices, care resources, and memory. A normal chatbot can discuss one fragment and then disappear. HealthIA is designed to carry unfinished health work forward until there is a durable, evidence-backed outcome.

This is the exact candidate running on Google Cloud. The patient signs in through the real HealthIA authentication flow. The interface follows the browser language, while clinical content follows the language the patient is actually using. The live runtime is Gemini 3.5 Flash on Vertex AI with Google ADK available for bounded agent execution, Firestore as canonical patient state, and private Cloud Storage for original evidence.

The patient opens Results and uploads a synthetic clinical PDF. HealthIA does something important before asking a model to interpret anything: it preserves the original bytes first in private Google Cloud Storage. That gives every later interpretation a recoverable source of truth.

Only after the original is safe does Gemini 3.5 Flash perform bounded multimodal extraction. HealthIA structures only what can be supported by the document. The result is committed to Firestore, linked to the original document, and connected to the patient’s longitudinal state with provenance. If the source cannot be interpreted reliably, the workflow fails closed rather than inventing a finding.

Now the patient asks HealthIA to explain the exact result that was just uploaded and confirm that the original was saved. This creates a Taskmaster mission. The mission is not marked complete merely because a language model produced a paragraph. HealthIA retrieves the persisted result and original-document metadata, attaches those evidence identifiers to the mission, and closes the task only when the requested durable outcome actually exists.

That distinction matters. Chat memory says, “we talked about a lab result.” Durable work says, “this exact result and this exact original document still exist, belong to this patient, and are linked to the completed mission.” HealthIA is built around the second model.

HealthIA can also turn understanding itself into a complete workflow. We call this HealthIA Explain. The patient asks for a private video explaining the glucose result. HealthIA resolves the language of that request, selects only authorized topic-relevant evidence, and asks Gemini 3.5 Flash for a structured education storyboard under a strict schema.

The narration is generated with Gemini 2.5 Pro Text-to-Speech, using a natural male voice. For visual concepts where motion genuinely helps, HealthIA can add one generic scene generated by Veo 3.1 Fast. The privacy boundary is deliberate: exact patient values remain on controlled HealthIA cards. Veo never receives the patient’s name, medications, laboratory values, identifiers, dates, or other patient-specific facts. Its prompt is generic and PHI-free.

The video is then assembled into a real MP4, stored privately under the patient’s own media path, attached to the same durable mission state, and made available inside HealthIA. If narration, visual generation, validation, or storage fails, HealthIA does not pretend that a video was created; the mission remains retryable.

The patient can play the completed explanation directly in the product. This is not a decorative media button. It is an agentic workflow with intent detection, evidence retrieval, planning, generation, validation, private storage, and a patient-visible outcome.

Finally, the patient logs out and signs in again. The result, original document, completed mission, and longitudinal state are still there. Continuity survives the browser session because Firestore remains the source of truth and the original clinical evidence remains in private Cloud Storage.

The backend shown here is the exact candidate on Cloud Run, using Gemini 3.5 Flash on Vertex AI, Google ADK, Firestore, private Cloud Storage, Secret Manager, Gemini 2.5 Pro Text-to-Speech, and Veo 3.1 Fast. HealthIA ONE does not try to replace clinical judgment. It uses AI where interpretation is valuable, deterministic logic where precision matters, and explicit boundaries where safety and human control matter most.

HealthIA ONE: your health never starts over.
